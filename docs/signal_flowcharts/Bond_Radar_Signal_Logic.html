SIG01 · Signal

Material drawing on a Cat A bank facility

Rule built · data gap
What it means
A client draws more than EUR 50m on any Cat A bank facility: an RCF, a term loan or a bilateral facility. The facility does not have to be labelled RCF.
Why DCM cares
When a company needs cash it usually draws on its bank lines first. A large drawing can precede refinancing in the bond market.
Rule
Drawing = drawn amount now minus drawn amount at the prior snapshot. Material if HSBC's drawing is at least EUR 50m, or if the inferred total (HSBC drawing / HSBC participation share) is. Only Cat A facilities count.
Data used
CLO sub-limits: drawn amount (two fields, compared separately), currency, sole-lender flag. Facilities: limit_cat for Cat A.
Status today
Built and tested. The default drawn field is blank in CLO, and no FX source or participation share has been supplied, so no drawing can be called material yet.
Open points
Authoritative drawn amount (LoanIQ), FX source and date, HSBC participation share, comparison period.

SIG02 · Signal

New RCF, term loan or bridge

Working
What it means
A new RCF, term loan or bridge facility, dated by the internal credit approval date.
Why DCM cares
New bank facilities, bridges in particular, are often refinanced in the bond market. The approval date gives an earlier signal than signing.
Rule
Event date = credit approval date from Application Ratings, linked through the credit proposal serial number. Candidate if approved in the last 90 days.
Data used
Facilities: facility, product, credit proposal serial, creation date, proposed limit. Application Ratings: approval date.
Status today
Working. In the three-client run, 9 facilities were approved within 90 days.
Open points
Credit team to confirm the approval relates to the new facility, not a later review. Product codes for RCF, term loan and bridge.

SIG03A · Signal

Swap roll on maturing debt

No source
What it means
A client rolls an existing swap that is tied to debt that is maturing.
Why DCM cares
Rolling the hedge on maturing debt points to refinancing of that debt.
Rule
Planned: swap linked to maturing debt and rolled or novated within the lookback. FX swaps first.
Data used
Needs derivatives trade data: trade ID, product, dates, notional, linked debt, roll flag.
Status today
No source identified, so no evidence.
Open points
Locate the derivatives trade source and its owner.

SIG03B · Signal

New pre-hedge swap enquiry

No source
What it means
A client asks HSBC about a new pre-hedge swap ahead of an issuance.
Why DCM cares
Pre-hedging, FX swaps in particular, is a strong sign that an issuance is being prepared.
Rule
Planned: an enquiry for a new swap, linked to new financing, within the lookback.
Data used
Needs enquiry or pricing-request records: client, date, product, status, notional, purpose.
Status today
The signal does not exist in markets systems today.
Open points
Decide on a source (Bloomberg chat was suggested) and the event definition.

Empty export
What it means
A new entity is onboarded, or KYC is refreshed for signatories.
Why DCM cares

Rule

SIG06 · Signal

Financial covenant breach or pressure

Rule built · no data
What it means
A covenant breach, or leverage within half a turn of the covenant maximum.
Why DCM cares
Covenant pressure can lead to refinancing, or to a hybrid transaction.
Rule
Headroom = maximum minus actual. Pressure when headroom is half a turn or less; breach when the condition fails.
Data used
Covenant tests: type, actual value, threshold, operator, test date, approval status.
Status today
Built and tested, but no genuine financial covenant data has been supplied.
Open points
Financial covenant source (net debt / EBITDA actual and maximum). The DRR covenant-waiver date is a lead to check.

Context

Facility utilisation

Context
What it means
How much of a facility is used: drawn amount divided by limit.
Why DCM cares
Supporting view of facility usage beside the signals.
Rule
Only when both amounts share currency, units and scope. Above 70% is highlighted.
Data used
DRR, CLO and PVD limits and utilisation.
Status today
In the three-client run, 5 ratios were above 70%.
Open points
Product and participation scope. Utilisation is not covenant pressure.

Context

Credit category and rating

Context
What it means
Each facility's credit category (Cat A, B or S) and the client's credit risk rating (CRR).
Why DCM cares
Cat A decides which drawings count for SIG01. The CRR gives credit context.
Rule
Final CRR with its scale; blank or N/A stays unavailable. Competing CRRs are kept side by side.
Data used
Facilities limit_cat and grades, PVD CRR, Application Ratings CRR.
Status today
In the three-client run, 18 CRRs were populated.
Open points
CRR scale and version meanings; definitions of CAT-A, B and S.
