Jerome explained the threshold requirements for RCF drawings as follows:
There are two thresholds to consider. The first is HSBC's own share of the drawing on the RCF, and the second is the total inferred or assumed drawing on the entire RCF. For example, if HSBC holds 10% of an RCF and their participation is drawn at 10 million euros, that means the total RCF drawing is 100 million euros.
Jerome stated that anything below 50 million euros in total drawing is considered insignificant for investment-grade corporates. So a minimum threshold of 50 million euros total drawing would be the baseline for flagging a relevant signal for DCM purposes.
Jerome also emphasized the importance of having a minimum threshold to filter out noise, giving an example that if a local subsidiary in India draws on a local facility for only 5 or 10 million euros, that would be irrelevant for DCM and should not trigger an alert.
