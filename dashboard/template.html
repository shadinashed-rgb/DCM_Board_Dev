Which internal signals point to a bond conversation?

Bond Radar brings HSBC's internal credit, facility and rating data together so DCM bankers can see which clients show early signs of a refinancing need. This board presents the signals extracted in the current development run for Vodafone, Siemens Energy and SoftBank — what each signal found, where the arithmetic tests fire, and which definitions still need an owner's decision before any signal can become a trigger.

    How to read this view

    HSBC corporate format. Start with the client matrix, open a client for the evidence behind each cell, then use the explorer and owner-review queue to work through what needs a decision.

    Evidence, not alerts
    All records are development evidence. business_trigger is blank for every record: a red cell means an arithmetic test fired under a development rule, not that a DCM opportunity has been confirmed.
    Grey is not zero
    SIG03 has no enquiry source and SIG04's KYC export is header-only. Missing, stale or empty data is shown in grey with a dash — it never establishes that a client had no activity.
    No silent arithmetic
    Amounts are shown in their native currency. Nothing is FX-converted or summed across sources, currencies, snapshots or parent/child facilities, and competing CRRs are kept side by side.
