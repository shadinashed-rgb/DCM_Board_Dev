Which internal signals point to a bond conversation?

Bond Radar brings HSBC's internal credit, facility and rating data together so DCM bankers can see which clients show early signs of a refinancing need. The signals now follow the definitions agreed with Jerome and the DCM team: any Cat A drawing over EUR 50m, new facilities dated by credit approval, two pre-hedging signals, covenant pressure within half a turn, and rating advisory from call reports. This board shows what each signal found for Vodafone, Siemens Energy and SoftBank, and what is still missing before any signal can become a trigger.

    How to read this view

    HSBC corporate format. Start with the client matrix, open a client for the evidence behind each cell, then use the explorer and owner-review queue to work through what needs a decision.

    Evidence, not alerts
    All records are development evidence. business_trigger is blank for every record: a red cell means a test agreed with DCM fired on development data, not that an opportunity has been confirmed. Each rule needs its owner's sign-off first.
    Grey is not zero
    SIG03A/B and SIG06 have no source, SIG07 needs CME call reports and SIG04's KYC export is header-only. Grey with a dash means missing data — never that a client had no activity.
    No silent arithmetic
    Amounts are shown in their native currency. Nothing is FX-converted or summed across sources, currencies, snapshots or parent/child facilities, and competing CRRs are kept side by side.
